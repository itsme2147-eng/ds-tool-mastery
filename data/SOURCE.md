# Data sources (all real, public)

| File | Description | Origin |
|------|-------------|--------|
| penguins.csv | 344 penguins from the Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica: species, island, bill/flipper measurements, body mass, sex | Palmer Station LTER; Gorman, Williams & Fraser (2014). Copy from https://github.com/mwaskom/seaborn-data |
| flights.csv | Monthly international airline passengers, 1949-1960 | Box & Jenkins classic time-series data. Copy from https://github.com/mwaskom/seaborn-data |
| (bundled) breast cancer | 569 digitised fine-needle-aspirate cases, 30 numeric features | UCI Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer (Wolberg, Street & Mangasarian); bundled with scikit-learn, no download needed |
